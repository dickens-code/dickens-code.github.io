rclone-g-photos

rclone-g-photos is a self-hosted, personal integration utility configured with Rclone to automate the transfer and backup of media files from local Synology NAS storage to Google Photos.

Application Overview

  • Purpose: Synchronizes personal photos and videos stored across nested local directories to Google Photos for backup and timeline browsing.
  • How It Works: Utilizes Rclone and official Google Photos API endpoints over authenticated OAuth 2.0 channels to transmit media items directly from local storage.
  • Data Architecture: Operates strictly on user-managed local systems (macOS / Synology DSM). No files, account details, or credentials are sent to or stored on external servers.
