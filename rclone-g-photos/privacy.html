Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NAS Photo Uploader handles your information and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Data We Access

The application only accesses local image and video files designated by the user on their Synology NAS, along with temporary OAuth 2.0 authentication tokens provided by Google.

2. Google API Scopes

We request the following permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.appendonly: Used strictly to upload selected media files to your Google Photos library. The application cannot read, edit, view, or delete any existing photos or albums in your Google Photos account.

3. Limited Use Compliance

  • Data access is used solely to provide local-to-cloud media upload functionality.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or commercial purposes.
  • No user data is used to train AI or machine learning models.

4. Data Storage

All authentication tokens and transfer logs are stored locally on your personal storage device. No information is collected, hosted, or logged on remote servers.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke access at any time via Google Security Settings.

6. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact: dickens.tam.bi@pulsar.com
