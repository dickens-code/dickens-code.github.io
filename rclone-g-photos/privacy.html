Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how rclone-g-photos processes user data and adheres to Google API Services User Data Policy standards.

1. Data Accessed

rclone-g-photos only accesses the local photo and video directories specified by the user on their Synology NAS or computer, along with OAuth 2.0 tokens issued by Google during initial setup.

2. Google API Permissions & Scopes

The application requests access to the following scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.appendonly: Used exclusively to upload local media files into the user's Google Photos account. rclone-g-photos cannot view, read, list, alter, or delete existing photos, albums, or media items in your Google account.

3. Limited Use Compliance

  • Data access is used exclusively to facilitate file backup to Google Photos.
  • We do not sell, share, trade, or transfer Google user data to third parties.
  • We do not utilize Google user data for advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking.
  • No user data or media content is used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All configuration settings and authentication tokens are kept strictly on the user's personal hardware within the local Rclone configuration. No data is stored, cached, or transmitted to any third-party infrastructure.

5. Revoking Permissions

Access can be disconnected at any time via Google Account Permissions.

6. Contact
