NAS Photo Uploader

NAS Photo Uploader is a self-hosted, personal automation utility built to synchronize media files from local Synology NAS storage to Google Photos.

Application Overview

  • Purpose: Automates photo and video archiving directly to Google Photos without requiring continuous manual browser uploads.
  • Mechanism: Scans local NAS directories, verifies file integrity, and uploads media items using the official Google Photos API.
  • Privacy First: Runs locally on user hardware. No media or user credentials are transmitted to or stored on external third-party servers.
